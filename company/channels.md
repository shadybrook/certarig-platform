# Channels

Listen in public. Draft in the repository. A person decides what leaves the building.

## Listen

Use these when today's brief needs a real question from someone who runs a bench. Quote a public sentence, name the page, and date the visit. Do not log in as anyone, do not create an account, and do not post.

- The repository itself: open issues and the README on `product/agent-lifecycle-2026-11-13`. The product's own limits are a source, not a competitor to route around.
- Makers and students who already publish a Pi, ADC, relay, or small hydraulic or thermal bench. Search recent public posts. A useful hit names a measurement, a trip, or a missing record.
- Instrumentation, mechatronics, and test-engineering course pages that publish a lab rig and a report requirement. The offer that fits is the simulator first, then a mapped bench, then a checksummed export.
- OctoPrint's public documentation and forum only as context for the VIRTUAL-printer lab. Twelve checks on OctoPrint 1.11.8 VIRTUAL are the whole claim. Do not tell a printer owner that CertaRig runs their machine.
- Public lab and makerspace pages in India when the reference-bench ceiling of ₹3,000 is the relevant fact. Name the page. Do not invent a contact.

Skip threads that ask for leaked exam content, pirated software, or a way around a safety interlock. CertaRig does not answer those.

## Draft, then stop

A draft may name a public page and the role of the person who published it. If that page prints an organizational address, cite the page. Do not collect personal inboxes from profiles, directories, or search results. Do not send the draft. The Monday and Thursday source file is a list for the owner to accept, rewrite, or ignore.

Posts for the owner to publish, if they choose:

- The GitHub repository, as a link from a short explanation of the kernel boundary.
- A course or lab note that can be handed to a lecturer: simulator first, no Pi required for the first hour.
- A single technical paragraph that cites one dated lab fact from [COMPANY.md](COMPANY.md).

Do not buy an audience, open a second account, or reply under a customer name. Do not post the same text in several communities on the same day.
