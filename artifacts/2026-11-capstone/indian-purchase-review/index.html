Simulation results and limits

926,711 finite recorded samples · ngspice 47 · full-horizon checks · offline replay. Expected rejections remain design findings, not proof that those parts are safe.

The integrated circuit extends the earlier R1 behavioral cutoff/watchdog/reset network. It is NOT the DRV8871 module schematic. It uses generic 100V switch/diode models, a bounded BLDC input load, a 14.4ohm valve (inferred from Uflow-family 12V/10W), and assumed valve inductance 0.2H/0.5H. Exact valve, driver, watchdog, gate-drive and clamp assembly remain unselected. The valve suppression model uses a 28.5V avalanche knee plus diode, not a measured SMBJ18A curve. Nominal integrated pump input assumes 1uF and no excess startup demand; the deliberate failed-start comparison assumes 100uF and 3A. Neither is published TOPSFLO internal data. The -1V pump reverse criterion is an engineering assumption, not a manufacturer tolerance. No mechanical closure time, hydraulic transient, valve position, safety integrity level or selected physical assembly is qualified.

Receiver and measurement changes

100Ω protected-input envelope passes modeled 21.6–26.4V and Pi-off cases. 120Ω × 33mA = 3.96V at the burden; this does not simulate the full SEN0262 buffer. A 12V supply lacks full-scale Nishka-loop compliance.

INA219 with 0.1Ω shunt cannot measure the old 4A threshold. 2.5A is measurable but actual pump startup and independent fast protection still need design review.

Power and hydraulic limits

DRV8871's 3.6A continuous retail claim fails the thermal analogy. Actual board cooling and auto-recovery behavior remain unqualified.

5–6m pump head means ~0.49–0.59bar static, not a physical operating target. The low-range sensor would overrange. A selected valve/bypass hydraulic transient model cannot be completed without their curves and closing behavior.
