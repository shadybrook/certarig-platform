What stays in the project

Reviewed rig interview and configuration; distinct thermal simulator; exact-profile rehearsal; human-approved deterministic execution; real calibrated sensing and measured electrical response; revalidation and complete offline-verifiable evidence.

The preferred physical increment remains one low-energy pump loop with an NC valve. The approved Phase3 defines the reusable commissioning workflow as the product and a water loop as a validation case.

04

Open/missing receiver burden

Contain the input voltage and detect the invalid calibrated reading. Keep authority refused after the signal recovers until an explicit reset. Restore the resistor and requalify before running.

Original unsafe control: rejected
Revised mitigation checks: passed

Evidence and remaining work

missing_burden_protected → receiver_burden_open_refused

A missing resistor is not made healthy by software. The guard exercised here is an offline reference, not deployed Edge integration.

05

DRV8871 advertised as 3.6A continuous

Use a one-direction power stage appropriate for an on/off pump/solenoid. A 0.154ohm hot bound and mounted thermal path no worse than 20C/W give 89.9C at 3.6A/50C ambient in the thermal analogy.

Original unsafe control: rejected
Revised mitigation checks: passed

Evidence and remaining work

drv8871_3.6A_continuous_claim → unidirectional_stage_3.6A_thermal

Vishay IRL540 0.077ohm at 5V gate is the reference; 2x hot multiplier and 20C/W are design bounds, not measured assembly properties. Gate drive, SOA, suppression and heatsinking remain to qualify.

06

Hot-board driver thermal failure

The same one-direction stage gives 56.9C at 1.5A and 50C ambient under those declared thermal bounds.

Original unsafe control: rejected
Revised mitigation checks: passed

Evidence and remaining work

drv8871_1.5A_hot_board_bound → unidirectional_stage_hot_1.5A

The DRV8871 control remains rejected. This demonstrates a different candidate design, not a retrofit qualification of that board.

07

Weak supply/wiring during startup

Specify adequate supply and total wiring/contact impedance, current-limit startup and sequence the valve after the pump settles. A 0.3ohm source envelope passes the previous weak-source startup test.

Original unsafe control: rejected
Revised mitigation checks: passed

Evidence and remaining work

pump_weak_supply_startup → supply_10.8V_0.3ohm_3x_start

The new weak-source scenario deliberately latches a refusal. Actual adapter current limit, wiring resistance and pump startup must be measured; nameplate wattage alone is insufficient.

08

Startup trips the old 2.5A latch

Use bounded soft start/current limiting; measure pump current across a correctly sized 50milliohm shunt; retain a 5A fast veto plus an approximately 80ms sustained-overload timer above 2.5A. Add output discharge and reset-required fault latching.

Original unsafe control: rejected
Revised mitigation checks: passed

Evidence and remaining work

integrated_unknown_startup_trip → softstart_3A_100uF

The 50milliohm circuit requires a suitable precision power shunt, Kelvin wiring and recalibration; an untouched R100 INA219 board is not this circuit. Actual eFuse SOA, fast response and pump internals remain unqualified.

Boundaries that remain

Power-stage safe operating area is still open. The modeled short imposes about 0.42J in the current-limiting pass element before latching. Device transient/SOA and actual PCB must support the entire waveform; the model does not prove that. It assumes a settled input rail before permit and omits adapter hot-plug/foldback. The receiver guard is an offline reference, not deployed Edge integration.

Pressure limits remain unfrozen. The exact water valve, its zero-differential service, pump revision/inrush and independent pressure protection still need documentation. Current/flow do not prove valve position. A gravity-fed design remains an optional separately reviewed alternative; live-provider qualification is a separate outstanding mission.
