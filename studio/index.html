System architecture

Reasoning is separated from execution and safety.

The browser presents evidence and recommendations. The service layer maintains the rig graph, policy decisions, plans, and diagnoses. A deterministic runtime owns state transitions. Independent safety remains outside the AI path.

Data flow

Every conclusion can be traced back to an input.

  1. 1Ingest

    Configuration, calibration, procedure, and telemetry records.

  2. 2Normalize

    Typed devices, channels, units, ranges, limits, and source references.

  3. 3Compare

    Version changes and the evidence or assumptions they invalidate.

  4. 4Plan

    Bounded checks with prerequisites, acceptance criteria, and abort rules.

  5. 5Execute

    Deterministic simulator states and measured responses.

  6. 6Report

    Outcome, diagnosis, evidence bundle, and items for human review.
