## Design

Admire the object first. Type arrives late, small, and only after the motion has already made the point.

One room. Warm floor light. Photographic plates of the bench, the latch, and three desks. No grid. No chapter numbers. No gold presentation cards.

The picture should behave like the product:

- A sentence walks toward **4.2 bar** and dies on the glass. Nothing crosses.
- Pressure writes itself as a strip chart. The lamp is on. The trace crosses the glass. The lamp goes out. Pressure comes home. The lamp stays out. The pin stays down.
- Light may travel Operator → Agent → Kernel. A glass notch sits before the rig.
- Six floor lamps recede into the room. The last one waits. Arm is a separate act.
- Commissioning questions are lights on someone else’s desks, not a bullet list.

If a beat can be labeled or demonstrated, demonstrate it.
